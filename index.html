About Me & My Passion

Acting is my ultimate future goal. For me, the stage is where I truly come alive. In fact, friends and family often say that I look way more real when acting compared to my day-to-day life. On stage, there is a script to follow with which I can experience being different characters in a controlled environment.

Right now, I am actively expanding my vocabulary of performance arts by attending an intensive acting workshop outside of school to foster and refine my natural talent.

Education & Training

I am currently a high school student balancing rigorous academics with intensive theatrical training. My foundational training comes from professional coaching outside of school hours and rookie experience in middle-school.

"Acting is not being someone else, but being yourself in unimaginable circumstances." — A guiding quote I live by.
